Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Valgus Creative ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / DSGVO / RGPD), and applicable European data protection standards. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, store, and safeguard your information across Kenya, Germany, France, and international markets.

1. Data Controller & Contact

The controller responsible for personal data processing on this website is Valgus Creative (operating as Valgus Designs), based in Westlands, Nairobi, Kenya. For privacy requests, data access, or deletion inquiries, contact us through our official contact channels or review our Impressum.

2. Information We Collect & Legal Basis

We process personal and technical data only where necessary and on lawful bases under GDPR (Art. 6(1)) and the Kenya Data Protection Act:

  • Inquiry & Feedback Data: Name, email address, phone number, selected services, or feedback submitted voluntarily via our contact and feedback forms (processed to respond to your request or perform pre-contractual steps).
  • Consent Preferences & Audit Log: Your consent choices (essential storage/core media vs. optional Google Fonts CDN and embedded third-party media), consent ID, action type, visited path, and SHA-256 hashed IP address and user agent recorded when you interact with our privacy banner (processed based on consent and legal compliance/accountability).
  • Server & Security Logs: Technical connection data such as IP address, browser type, operating system, timestamps, and requested pages processed to maintain security, prevent abuse, and deliver pages reliably.

3. Cookies, Local Storage, Cloudinary & External Fonts

We use essential browser storage and cookies required for core site functionality, security, and remembering your privacy preferences. Essential website and portfolio images are delivered through Cloudinary as a media delivery processor (with automatic format and quality optimization, without advertising or analytics tracking cookies) so visual content renders reliably. Optional external resources—specifically Google Fonts CDN for typography and any embedded third-party media—are gated behind your consent choices in our privacy banner, which you can update anytime via Privacy Settings in the footer.

4. Data Sharing & International Transfers

We do not sell your personal data. Data may be shared only with infrastructure, email, or media processors strictly necessary to operate our site and respond to inquiries, or where required by law. Where personal data is transferred outside the European Economic Area (EEA)—such as to Kenya or third-party cloud providers—we rely on appropriate safeguards, including Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) where applicable, alongside data minimization such as hashing IP addresses in consent logs.

5. Data Retention

We retain inquiry records, feedback, and consent logs only for as long as needed to fulfill the purpose for which they were collected, manage client communications, or satisfy legal and compliance obligations, after which data is deleted or anonymized.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you have the right to request access to, rectification of, or erasure of your personal data; restrict or object to processing; request data portability; withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior lawful processing; and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (such as the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in Kenya, a German state data protection authority, or the CNIL in France).

7. Security Measures

We apply technical and organizational security controls—including encrypted transport (HTTPS), restricted access, input validation, and hashed identifiers in consent records—to protect personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our services or legal requirements. The updated effective date will appear at the top of this page.
